A lowland of prairie and oak

The Willamette Valley is a rolling plain of terraces and broad floodplains, with scattered hills and foothills. Historically, open grasslands and oak savannas met wet prairies, wetlands, and forest along the rivers. Today, deep fertile soils and a mild climate support one of Oregon’s most productive agricultural regions.

WHAT SETS IT APART

Between coast and cascade

To the west · Coast Range
The valley is lower, gentler, and generally drier. The Coast Range rises into wetter, forested mountains.

To the east · Cascades
The valley’s warm, broad floodplain gives way to steeper, higher terrain with cooler conditions and greater precipitation.
